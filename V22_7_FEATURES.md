# ZenithMax V22.7 — Creator Ecosystem

V22.7 broadens ZenithMax around the categories of functionality users expect from modern video/creator platforms.

## Creator
- Creator Hub
- Video publishing workflow
- Shorts + long-form
- Playlists + ordered Series
- Stories
- Live scheduling
- Premieres
- Sounds
- Effects presets
- Remix / Duet / Stitch drafts
- Polls / Q&A
- Creator analytics
- Demo memberships, tips, subscriptions and campaigns

## Viewer
- Personalized recommendations
- Trending
- Following feed
- Unified search
- Watch history
- Saved videos/posts
- Comments, likes, shares
- Notifications

## Community / communication
- Social posts
- Stories
- Direct messaging
- Voice notes
- Group chat
- Audio/video calls

## Other ZenithMax product areas retained
- Music
- Games
- Follower rankings
- Responsive phone/tablet/desktop layouts
- CEO account recognition
- Resumable uploads
- Remote licensed media
- Moderation/reporting

## Important implementation boundaries
Actual live broadcasting still needs a production WebRTC/RTMP media transport. Real monetization requires a proper payment provider, business/legal review, tax handling, and age-appropriate adult supervision where applicable.
